# Dami portfolio | Editorial system

The site keeps the restrained editorial system established from the user's [Hex](https://hex.tech/) reference. The latest supplied desktop and mobile portrait layout directs the homepage hero. It uses Dami's own name, portrait, work, and content.

## First viewport

The existing five-link navigation and theme switch remain. The homepage opens with a centered “Hi, I’m Dami.” introduction, two short paragraphs about his full-stack work, and direct project and contact links. Dami's portrait sits alongside the copy with his confirmed role, County Dublin location, and availability beneath it. Mobile stacks the introduction and portrait without reserving an empty viewport.

## Page system

Home, Projects, Articles, About, and Contact share one header and footer. Home has the portrait introduction followed by a contact form. About keeps the verified profile without repeating the photo. Projects are presented as self-contained cards with purpose, contribution, technologies, and direct public links. There are no case-study pages. Articles lists Dami's published Medium writing.

About keeps the verified profile and experience, then presents the evidenced stack as a loose, floating field of official technology marks on desktop. On mobile, the same content becomes a readable three-column icon grid with names and roles.

## Visual rules

- Warm white canvas, near-black plum ink, delicate cool rules, and a restrained pale rose accent.
- Fine grid and dot texture remain on the shared navigation and secondary pages; the portrait hero uses a plain canvas.
- Manrope is the sole typeface across headings, body copy, navigation, projects, skills, and contact. Regular display weight and restrained tracking keep the large headings clear; medium to semibold weight gives controls and labels definition. Captions remain readable on mobile.
- The main navigation uses plain, readable text with an underline for the active page. Two restrained rectangular links in the homepage hero lead to Projects and the contact form.
- Project cards use a restrained light surface, large Manrope titles, compact technology labels, and direct repository or live links, following the supplied mobile reference without its case-study action.
- Generous whitespace, one-time scroll entrances for selected content, and clear focus states. A brief page-opening indicator makes navigation state visible. Framer Motion animates entrances and hover feedback; Lenis smooths wheel scrolling. Reduced-motion settings remove spatial movement. Mobile keeps the typography expressive without clipping or horizontal scrolling.
- The portfolio remains a developer site: no borrowed SaaS navigation, dashboards, metrics, or product claims.
